# An owner answers a join request

Any member can invite a person into an org, but only an owner can approve a
join request. The two acts end the same way, with a new member, so the
difference looks like an oversight. It is not. An invitation is a member
vouching for somebody they know and naming them by email. A join request comes
from a person the org may never have heard of, who found the org's name in the
directory. The org's owners decide who gets in that way.

Every member still sees the waiting requests on the members page, so a member
who knows the person can tell an owner, or invite them, which is the road that
is theirs to take.

A declined request stays declined, and the person cannot ask that org again.
That keeps an org from hearing from the same stranger every day. Becoming a
member, by an invitation or an approval, clears the request, so a person who is
later taken out of the org is not held to an old decline.

## Considered and rejected

**Make invitations owner-only too.** One rule for every road in, at the cost of
taking from members a power they use to bring in people they know.
